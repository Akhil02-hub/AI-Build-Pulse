About AI Engineer Pulse & FAQ

AI Engineer Pulse is a 100% free AI news aggregator that collects, filters, and categorizes the latest AI industry news every 24 hours. It is specifically designed for AI Engineers, Machine Learning practitioners, and software developers who want to stay updated without a paywall.

Is this AI news really free?

Yes! AI Engineer Pulse is completely free to use. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, and no hidden fees. We believe AI engineering news should be accessible to everyone.

What kind of news does this app show?

We aggregate news related to LLM Engineering, RAG, AI Agents, Model Training, Inference, AI Infrastructure, MLOps, and Multimodal AI.

How often is the data updated?

The system runs an automated GitHub Action every midnight (IST) to fetch the latest articles, keeping the feed fresh and relevant.
