AI ENGINEER PULSE
Daily AI Industry Intelligence
https://aiengineerdaily.vercel.app/

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------
AI Engineer Pulse is a daily news dashboard for people who build with AI.
Instead of scrolling ten different feeds every morning, you open one page and
get the AI engineering stories from the last 24 hours, already sorted by
category.

It is not a general tech news site. It is deliberately narrow: LLM engineering,
RAG, AI agents, model training, inference, AI infrastructure, MLOps, and
multimodal AI. If it doesn't help you ship or understand an AI system, it
usually doesn't make the cut.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHO IT'S FOR
------------------------------------------------------------
- AI engineers and ML practitioners keeping up without losing a morning
- Researchers who want the applied signal, not just the papers
- Technical founders and platform teams tracking tooling and infra

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT'S INSIDE
------------------------------------------------------------
Today's Overview   The last 24 hours. Searchable, filterable, refreshable.
Weekly Overview    The week rolled up, for spotting direction over noise.
Briefing           A short narrative digest — the "read this first" summary.
Research           Papers and technical write-ups worth your time.
Tools              Frameworks, SDKs, and utilities that just shipped.
Trends             Which topics are heating up across the aggregated feed.
Reading List       Save things now, read them later (requires an account).

Also included: a search box, category filters, light/dark themes, and a
built-in chatbot that answers questions about the site and searches the news.

------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT WORKS
------------------------------------------------------------
Every day at midnight IST, an automated GitHub Action fetches fresh articles,
filters them against the engineering-focused categories, and updates the feed.
If the data ever goes stale, the app shows a banner so you know what you're
looking at.

The site is a static single-page app backed by Supabase for authentication and
data. Accounts are optional — browsing never requires one. Sign-in is email and
password, with a security question (first pet, birth city, or high school) so
you can reset your own password without waiting on support.

No paywall. No ads. No account needed to read.
